# Module Outline: How to Deliver a Module

## Brief Overview

This module walks participants through the end-to-end delivery workflow for a single Roadshow module. It covers the supporting materials available (slide decks, presenter notes, Showroom lab), the recommended cadence of live walkthrough followed by student work time, and how to conduct reviews. Participants practice the workflow so they can replicate it when delivering to customers.

## Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Solution architects, specialist solution architects, adoption architects, technical account managers, sales specialists
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Completion of Modules 1-2 (understanding of Roadshow structure and how to position it)
- **Estimated duration:** 30 minutes

## Learning Objectives

- Identify the supporting materials available for each Roadshow module (slide deck, presenter notes, Showroom lab environment)
- Demonstrate the four-phase delivery workflow: introduction with slides, live walkthrough in the lab, student hands-on work time, and review
- Apply pacing techniques to keep a module on schedule within its allocated time
- Handle common delivery situations such as student questions, environment issues, and mixed-experience audiences

## Detailed Steps

1. Navigate to the Roadshow's supporting materials in the Showroom interface: locate the slide deck, presenter notes, and lab instructions for a sample module.
2. Review the presenter notes and identify the key talking points for the module introduction.
3. Walk through the four-phase delivery workflow:
   a. **Introduction:** Present the slides covering the module's topic and learning objectives.
   b. **Live walkthrough:** Demonstrate the first exercise in the lab environment while narrating each step.
   c. **Student work time:** Allow participants to complete the remaining exercises independently.
   d. **Review:** Reconvene and walk through the expected outcomes, answer questions, and highlight key takeaways.
4. Practice transitioning between each phase, noting the recommended time split (roughly 20% intro, 30% walkthrough, 35% student work, 15% review).
5. Review pacing guidelines: how to adjust if running ahead or behind schedule.
6. Read through common delivery situations and recommended responses:
   a. A student's environment is not responding.
   b. A student finishes early.
   c. The audience has mixed experience levels.
   d. A question falls outside the module's scope.
7. Answer the checkpoint questions to confirm understanding of the delivery workflow and pacing strategies.

## Key Takeaways

- Every Roadshow module follows the same four-phase delivery workflow, giving presenters a consistent pattern to rely on.
- Supporting materials (slides, presenter notes, lab) are designed to work together; presenters should review all three before delivering.
- Pacing is critical in a time-boxed workshop; the recommended time split keeps each phase productive without rushing.
- Preparing for common delivery situations reduces stress and helps maintain a professional, smooth session.

## Infrastructure Notes

Participants access the Showroom interface to locate supporting materials. The sample module used for the walkthrough exercise should be pre-loaded in the Showroom environment. No direct cluster interaction is required in this module; the focus is on the delivery process rather than the technical content itself.
