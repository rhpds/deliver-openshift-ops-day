# Module Outline: What Is the OpenShift Roadshow?

## Brief Overview

This module introduces the OpenShift Ops Day Roadshow: what it is, why it exists, and how it is structured. Participants learn about the Roadshow's value propositions for customers and the different delivery formats available. The module also provides a high-level tour of all 18 modules in the workshop so that presenters understand the full scope of content they can draw from.

## Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Solution architects, specialist solution architects, adoption architects, technical account managers, sales specialists
- **Prerequisites for this module:** None beyond general OpenShift awareness
- **Estimated duration:** 15 minutes

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the purpose and value propositions of the OpenShift Ops Day Roadshow
- Identify the three delivery formats (single-day workshop, regional event, individual sessions)
- List the major topic areas covered across the Roadshow's 18 modules
- Describe the high-level background and purpose of each of the 18 modules

## Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1       | Roadshow Purpose and Value Propositions | 5 min |
| 2       | Delivery Formats | 5 min |
| 3       | Tour of All 18 Modules | 5 min |

## Detailed Steps

1. Read the overview of the OpenShift Ops Day Roadshow, including its goals and the customer outcomes it targets.
2. Review the three delivery formats: single-day on-site workshop, multi-session regional event, and standalone individual sessions.
3. For each delivery format, note the recommended number of modules, total time, and ideal audience size.
4. Review the complete list of 18 Roadshow modules grouped by topic area.
5. For each module, read the one-sentence summary of its background and purpose.
6. Identify which modules align with common customer priorities (operations, security, networking, storage, observability, application management).
7. Answer the checkpoint questions to confirm understanding of the Roadshow structure and module landscape.

## Key Takeaways

- The Roadshow is a flexible, modular workshop designed to accelerate OpenShift adoption for operations teams.
- Three delivery formats let presenters adapt to different customer contexts and time constraints.
- The 18 modules span a wide range of Day 2 operations topics; presenters do not need to deliver all of them at once.
- Understanding the full module catalog helps presenters recommend the right sessions for a given customer's needs.
