# Module Outline: Positioning and Scheduling the Roadshow

## Brief Overview

This module covers how to position the OpenShift Ops Day Roadshow for different customer scenarios and how to schedule sessions effectively. Participants learn to assess a customer's operational maturity, select appropriate modules, and build an agenda that fits the chosen delivery format. The goal is to prepare field teams to propose and plan a Roadshow engagement confidently.

## Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Solution architects, specialist solution architects, adoption architects, technical account managers, sales specialists
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Completion of Module 1 (familiarity with the Roadshow structure and module catalog)
- **Estimated duration:** 15 minutes

## Learning Objectives

- Assess a customer's OpenShift operational maturity to determine which Roadshow modules are most relevant
- Position the Roadshow for three scenarios: single-day workshop, regional event, and individual sessions
- Build a sample agenda by selecting modules, ordering them logically, and allocating time
- Identify the logistics required to schedule and confirm a Roadshow engagement (RHDP environment, calendar, stakeholders)

## Detailed Steps

1. Review the customer assessment criteria: operational maturity level, current OpenShift usage, and stated pain points.
2. Using a provided scenario, identify which Roadshow modules address the customer's priorities.
3. Select modules for a single-day workshop agenda and arrange them in a logical sequence.
4. Adjust the agenda for a regional event format (multiple shorter sessions across several days).
5. Review the checklist for scheduling logistics: RHDP environment provisioning, calendar invitations, stakeholder communication, and pre-event preparation.
6. Note the lead times required for environment provisioning through the Red Hat Demo Platform.
7. Answer the checkpoint questions to confirm understanding of positioning and scheduling decisions.

## Key Takeaways

- Module selection should be driven by the customer's operational maturity and stated priorities, not by a fixed agenda.
- Each delivery format has different constraints; the agenda must account for available time, audience size, and session breaks.
- Scheduling requires coordination across RHDP environment provisioning, customer stakeholders, and presenter availability.
- A well-positioned Roadshow directly addresses customer pain points, increasing engagement and follow-up opportunities.
