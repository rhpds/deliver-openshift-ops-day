# Module Outline: Deliver the Workshop

## Brief Overview

This module covers the end-to-end process of delivering the OpenShift Roadshow to customers, from initial proposal through delivery and follow-up. Participants learn how to scope content, use the Roadshow materials, run a workshop, deliver individual modules, manage lab environments, prepare as a presenter, and include additional customer-specific content.

## Audience and Time

- **Target personas:** Solution architects, specialist solution architects, adoption architects, technical account managers, sales specialists
- **Prerequisites for this module:** None beyond general OpenShift awareness
- **Estimated duration:** 15 minutes

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the process for proposing, scoping, and scheduling a Roadshow delivery
- Explain the different ways to use Roadshow content (single unit, individual units, foundational vs. layered)
- Demonstrate the delivery workflow for a module including supporting materials, live walkthrough, student work, and reviews
- Describe how to provision and troubleshoot lab environments
- Identify how to prepare as a presenter and how to include additional customer-specific content

## Detailed Steps

### How to deliver the workshop to a customer

1. Describe the proposal process through the account team.
2. Explain how to scope content and time available for the engagement.
3. Describe how to secure presenters for the workshop.
4. Explain how to schedule delivery dates.

### How to use the OpenShift Roadshow content

5. Describe using the Roadshow as a single unit.
6. Describe using the Roadshow as a collection of individual units.
7. Explain the distinction between foundational modules and modules on layered products.

### How to run an OpenShift Roadshow workshop

8. Describe how to find presenters.
9. Explain how to schedule on RHDP.

### How to deliver a module

10. Review the supporting materials available for each module.
11. Describe the live walkthrough and discussion phase.
12. Describe the independent student work time phase.
13. Describe the review phase.

### How to prepare and handle the lab environments

14. Explain how to request and provision environments for many users.
15. Describe what the lab environment provides to users.
16. Identify known issues that arise and how to troubleshoot them.

### How a presenter should prepare to deliver content

17. Explain the process: request an environment and work through the content.

### How to include additional content

18. Describe how to incorporate customer-specific investigations.
19. Describe how to incorporate custom technical items.

## Key Takeaways

- Delivering the Roadshow starts with the account team and requires scoping content to the customer's needs and available time.
- Roadshow content is flexible — it can be delivered as one unit or as individual modules, mixing foundational and layered product content.
- Every module follows the same delivery pattern: supporting materials, live walkthrough, student work time, and reviews.
- Presenters must prepare by working through the content in a real environment before delivering to customers.
- Lab environments require advance provisioning and presenters should be aware of known issues and troubleshooting steps.

## Infrastructure Notes

No cluster access is required for this module. All content is informational and delivered through the Showroom interface.
